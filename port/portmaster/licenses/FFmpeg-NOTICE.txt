RECry plays the game's Bink cutscenes with FFmpeg 6.1.2 (https://ffmpeg.org),
licensed under the GNU LGPL 2.1 or later (see FFmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt). The
libraries in libs.aarch64/ are unmodified upstream source, built as shared
libraries with only the file protocol, the Bink demuxer, the Bink video/audio
decoders and libswresample enabled (configuration: tools/build.sh in the
RECry source). They are linked dynamically and can be replaced with any
compatible build of the same major versions.
